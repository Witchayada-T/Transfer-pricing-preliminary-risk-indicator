# Sharing and confidentiality

The public materials explain the tool’s purpose and intended use. The underlying model, source code, implementation details and collected company-level material are confidential and are not part of the public-facing edition.

Individual public financial pages are different from a ready-to-use, reusable research dataset. The study assembled its own records through local collection and audit. New company figures and commercial explanations supplied by reviewers may be confidential.

A functioning offline application necessarily carries executable rules and learned parameters internally. Keeping separate source/model files off a public page does not guarantee that a distributed executable cannot be inspected or reverse-engineered. Strict confidentiality may therefore require controlled distribution to authorised reviewers rather than an unrestricted public download.

No online model service is operated by this repository. For the professional background, consult the OECD guidelines and the applicable Thai transfer-pricing law through official sources.
